About Me

I am a Geomatics Engineering student at Institut Teknologi Sepuluh Nopember (ITS) with practical experience in surveying, mapping, GNSS, hydrographic surveying, UAV photogrammetry, and geospatial data processing. I have developed strong technical, analytical, leadership, and teamwork skills through academic projects, internships, and organizational involvement. I am eager to apply my knowledge and continue growing as a geospatial professional.

Key Strengths

  • Strong knowledge of surveying and geospatial data processing.
  • Experience in terrestrial, topographic, GNSS, hydrographic, and aerial surveys.
  • Proficient in operating Total Station, Waterpass, GNSS Static, and RTK systems.
  • Experienced in UAV-based aerial mapping and orthophoto generation.
  • Leadership experience in academic and organizational activities.
  • Strong teamwork, communication, and public speaking skills.
  • Good project planning, coordination, and time management abilities.
  • Detail-oriented, responsible, adaptable, and quick to learn.
